    The agreement comes near the end of Convert, once the AM has applied and BO has set the IB up.

    KYC, not a deposit. An IB must pass KYC before bringing in clients, so we know they're a real person. They don't need to make a deposit (OP, 11 Nov).

    What it says, in plain words

    The small tag after each point is the clause number, so you can check the exact wording.

    Who they are to us

    • A referrer, nothing more. They bring clients to us, but they're not our employee, agent or partner, and they pay their own taxes. 2c, 3b
    • They sign as a person, with their ID or passport number, or as a company, with its registration number. Parties
    • We decide whether to accept each client they bring in. 2e
    • They only earn on clients they introduced before the client opened an account with us. 3e
    • A client of theirs with no trades for over 12 months may stop counting as theirs. 2f

    Person to company. To move an IB from a person to a company later, the AM opens a case with BO on the Operations Service Desk, as an Onboarding Request with the type "KYC related" (OP, 11 Nov).

    How they get paid

    • Their rate is the one in their IB portal, under IB Profile, Rebate Rate Setting. We can change it with 10 days' notice. 6c
    • Rebates go into their IB account the next business day after the trade. Friday's arrive on Monday, and a holiday pushes it to the next business day. 6a
    • Amounts are rounded to 2 decimal places, and anything under 0.005 isn't paid. 6b
    • The smallest withdrawal from the IB account is 100, in the account's currency, unless they're closing the account. 4j
    • It's up to them to check their client links and rates. We only correct rebates for the last 30 days of trading. 4c
    • We can hold rebates during a client complaint or dispute, and hold back pay or close accounts if we suspect abuse, such as opening and closing trades just to earn rebates. 6d to 6f

    What they must and mustn't do

    They mustn't

    • Publish any ad or marketing that mentions TMGM without our OK, and they stop if we take our OK back. 4f
    • Say or suggest they're more than a referrer, such as our authorised representative. 2c
    • Use TMGM, Trademax or Trademax Global in a business or website name. 2d
    • Give financial advice or manage clients' money. 3d
    • Speak to the press for us. 4g
    • Do or say anything that harms our name, even after the agreement ends. 2b

    They must

    • Tell their clients about any fees or benefits they get from us. 3f
    • Follow our policies, let us check their activity, and tell us at once about any complaint or investigation against them. 4b, 4e, 4h
    • Keep our information confidential, even after the agreement ends. 10
    • Pay for any marketing they run on their own. In turn, we give them any materials we want them to use. 2g, 5a

    Changing or ending it

    • We can change the agreement with 5 business days' notice. If they disagree, they can end it. 7a
    • Either side can end it with 7 days' written notice. 7c
    • We can end it at once, for example if they break the law, abuse the program, seriously break the agreement, or claim to be more than a referrer. 7b
    • When it ends, they stop promoting us, hand back our materials and information, settle anything they owe us, and ask for any rebates still owed. 7e, 10d
    • The law of Vanuatu applies. 9
